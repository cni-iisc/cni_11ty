Generative AI Hackathon

Learn, Explore, Build Gen AI Solutions for India

{ PosterImage }

In this hackathon, you are encouraged to build an AI-driven solution that harnesses the power of IBM open-source technologies & also solutions to help with driving efficiency, automation, and innovation into business, any IT, any scientific solutions & operations. You can know more about all offered IBM Granite models and about their capabilities from here.

Some of the problem areas & tracks where you can participate with your novel ideas and concepts are listed as the below:

  • Track 1:

    Crop identification & using satellite observations is an age-old problem and remains challenging, especially when trying to generalize model predictions across India's fragmented small farms with diverse cropping practices. This situation is different from regions like the USA or Europe, where farms are larger and practices are more homogeneous. Therefore, there is a significant opportunity to contribute by using geospatial foundation models as a backbone, fine-tuned with Indian native satellite observations and field-collected label data for a variety of crops. Worth to note that Earth Observation (EO), weather, and climate (WxC) data present unique challenges due to their heterogeneity, like varying spectral bands and resolutions, and often require specialized domain knowledge.

    With TerraTorch - a fine-tuning and benchmarking toolkit for Geospatial Foundation Models built on PyTorch Lightning and tailored for satellite, weather, and climate data – one can build a custom model to classify crop types. You can read about the latest work from this reference paper here.

  • Track 2:

    Accurate short-term load forecasting is crucial for decarbonizing buildings, which contribute about one-third of global energy use and emissions. This task focuses on forecasting and anomaly detection for energy consumption in commercial and residential buildings, supporting efficient energy management, renewable integration, and reduced reliance on fossil fuels. You will work with pre-trained Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) that learn generalizable temporal patterns across domains, enabling accurate forecasts and anomaly detection for new buildings without retraining. You can also fine-tune these models for specific building types or operational needs. You can read all about the latest work from this reference paper here.   • Track 3:

    In this track, you can explore novel set of use-case scenarios which can leverage Multivariate time-series analysis and various IBM Granite models. Wherein e2e automation can be demonstrated to bring performance, customer experience, cost specific efficiencies. You are free to think about real-world issues and apply knowledge on Generative models, their integration with Agentic applications to automate different workflows targeting e2e user scenarios. Use-cases can be motivated or driven from India centric relevant scenarios. IBM has got a rich set of open tech assets and platform to build Agentic Applications for extracting, classifying, and summarising documents using Gen AI LLMs, build language detection and translation system using IBM watsonx.ai, automate workflows using Tool calling capabilities of LLMs. To get a view on variety of use-case scenarios you may go through Granite workshop here.
