Since joining HP Labs in 1998 after earning his Ph.D. from the University of Southern California, he has driven innovations in software-defined networking (SDN), GPU virtualization, container orchestration, edge computing, Private 5G, and AI/ML systems, enabling major technology transfers across HPE business units.

He is an IEEE Fellow, ACM Distinguished Scientist, and two-time Tsinghua AI 2000 Most Influential Scholar. He earned his B.Tech from IIT Delhi.
