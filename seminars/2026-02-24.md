Topic_abstract: "This talk posits a new quantum framework for designing and analyzing communication-efficient distributed quantum algorithms. Using this framework, we present distributed quantum algorithms for two fundamental problems in distributed computing, namely, leader election and agreement. Our quantum algorithms are significantly more message-efficient compared to their classical counterparts and breach the classical lower bounds. Joint work with Fabien Dufoulon (Lancaster University) and Frédéric Magniez (Université Paris Cité, CNRS, IRIF)."
