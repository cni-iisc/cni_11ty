Topic_abstract: "AI, enabled by impressive advances in semiconductors, has started to transform industries and societies. In this talk we will begin by exploring the AI eco-system, the challenges and opportunities and highlight why system knowledge is critical for leadership. Next, we will dive deeper into semiconductor advances as it relates to AI, including the eco-system dependencies, evolution of transistors, memory, computational architectures, interconnects, and packaging. Finally, we will summarize the impacts of these advances on R&D costs. The talk concludes by summarizing what is essential for product development and a personal view on opportunities for Indian product companies."
