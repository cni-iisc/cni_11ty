Topic_abstract: "We consider the problem of non-stationary reinforcement learning (RL) in the infinite-horizon average-reward setting. We model it by a Markov Decision Process with time-varying rewards and transition probabilities. Existing non-stationary RL algorithms focus on model-based and model-free value-based methods. Policy-based methods, despite their flexibility in practice are not theoretically well understood in non-stationary RL. We propose and analyze the first model-free policy-based algorithm, Non-Stationary Natural Actor-Critic (NS-NAC), a policy gradient method with a restart-based exploration for change and a novel interpretation of learning rates as adapting factors. Further, we present a bandit-over-RL-based parameter-free algorithm, BORL-NS-NAC, that does not require prior knowledge of the variation budget."
