Topic_abstract:  Let $\bA\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ be an arbitrary, known matrix and $\be$ a $q$-sparse adversarial vector. Given $\by=\bA\xS+\be$ and $q$, we seek the smallest robust solution set containing $\xS$ that is uniformly recoverable from $\by$ without knowing $\be.$  While exact recovery of $\xS$ via strong (and often impractical) structural assumptions on $\bA$ or $\xS$ (e.g., restricted isometry, sparsity) is well studied, recoverability for arbitrary $\bA$ and $\xS$ remains open. Our main result shows that the smallest robust solution set is $\xS + \ker(\bU), $  where $\bU$ is the unique projection matrix onto the intersection of rowspaces of all possible submatrices of $\bA$ obtained by deleting $2q$ rows. Moreover, we prove that every $\bx$ that minimizes the $\ell_0$-norm of $\by - \bA \bx$ lies in $\xS + \ker(\bU)$, which then gives a constructive approach to recover this set.
