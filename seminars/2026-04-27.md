Topic_abstract: "As a scientist who models how infectious diseases spread, understanding the usefulness of models in helping frame better health policy is important to me. Some years ago, we began to develop BharatSim, India's first ultra-large-scale agent-based simulation, initially in the context of understanding COVID-19 spread. More recently, we've demonstrated the use of BharatSim in understanding specific questions of direct policy relevance: when to open schools in the background of a pandemic, what interventions might work in the case of a bird-flu spillover event into humans, the nature of a potential mpox epidemic as well as the control of typhoid spread in urban contexts.  I'll talk broadly about the importance of good models and what should go into their construction, as well as provide illustrations from my own work, in particular BharatSim."
