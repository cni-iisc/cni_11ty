Topic_abstract: "Antenna design has traditionally relied on physical intuition, electromagnetic simulation, and extensive parametric studies. Recent advances in artificial intelligence are transforming this approach, enabling faster optimisation, surrogate modelling, inverse design, and, increasingly, the generation of novel antenna geometries.  This talk will review the evolution from conventional simulation-driven design to machine-learning-assisted and generative AI approaches, with examples covering a dielectric resonant antenna (DRA) and a mobile base-station antenna. The opportunities and challenges of AI-driven antenna design will be discussed, including physics integration, data requirements, generalisation, and reliable verification. Finally, future directions towards intelligent and autonomous antenna design will be explored. "
