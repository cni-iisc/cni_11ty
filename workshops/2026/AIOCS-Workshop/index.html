AI in Oral Cancer Screening Workshop: Bridging Innovation and Clinical Practice

AI in Oral Cancer Screening: Bridging Innovation and Clinical Practice is a one-day workshop bringing together researchers, clinicians, and startups working on AI-driven point-of-care screening for oral cancer, for technical talks and discussion on translating research into clinical impact.

Organized by

Indian Institute of Science and Centre for Networked Intelligence

🗓️ Date

Friday, July 31, 2026 (08:00am–02:00pm)

🏫 Mode

Offline
