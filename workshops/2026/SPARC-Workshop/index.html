SPARC Workshop on Distributed Learning and Optimization

The SPARC Workshop on Distributed Learning and Optimization is a one-day workshop featuring invited talks on recent advances in distributed learning, optimization, and related areas, bringing together researchers and students for technical discussions and research exchange.
