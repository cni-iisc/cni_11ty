CodeIT: CNI Workshop on Codes, Sequences and Information Theory

CodeIT 2025

Welcome to the CodeIT 2025 Workshop organized at IISc on October 11 and October 12, 2025. This event brings together researchers and practitioners in the fields of coding theory, sequence design, and information theory to celebrate Prof. P. Vijay Kumar and discuss recent advancements in the field. Join us for two days of insightful talks and engaging discussions.

Venue

In-person participation: Golden Jubilee Hall, ECE department, IISc. (📌 Map location)

Online participation: Meeting link will be shared with registered participants.
