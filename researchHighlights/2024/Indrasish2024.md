Identification of Broad Research Area: We have identified Channel Charting (CC) and
Statistical Channel State Information (S-CSI) as two broad areas of research interest. Our
goal is to explore potential methodologies to establish a meaningful connection between these
two domains.
<br>
<b>Literature Survey:</b> I have conducted several literature surveys on the aforementioned topics.
The existing body of work on Channel Charting spans a wide spectrum, beginning with its
foundational concepts, and extending to techniques for distance learning and feature
construction. These methodologies are designed to accurately predict the future
pseudo-positions of user terminals (UTs) within a cellular network. Moreover, several studies
explore the use of channel charting for predicting future channel state information (CSI).
These works also highlight its potential in reducing the channel estimation overheads.
As for Statistical CSI, my literature review primarily focuses on scenarios involving
reconfigurable intelligent surface (RIS)-aided massive MIMO systems. These works propose
rate maximization frameworks that rely solely on statistical CSI, effectively decreasing the
substantial overhead associated with instantaneous channel estimation.
We are also exploring potential problems in which not only the statistical properties of the
desired channels are known, but also the statistics of interference originating from
neighboring base stations. The objective is to understand how the availability of interference
statistics influences and potentially enhances existing approaches in the literature.
