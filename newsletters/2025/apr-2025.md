<td class="mceTextBlockContainer" style="padding: 12px 24px 12px 24px;">

<div id="dataBlockId-266" class="mceText" style="width: 100%;" data-block-id="266">

<p class="mcePastedContent"><span style="font-family: Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif;">CNI PhD Scholar, </span><a href="https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=cEY7NM4AAAAJ&amp;hl=en" target="\_blank"><span style="font-family: Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif;">Indrasish Chakraborty, </span></a><span style="font-family: Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif;">has developed an innovative communication framework that integrates Simultaneously Transmitting and Reflecting (STAR) Intelligent Reflecting Surfaces (IRS) with Orthogonal Time Frequency Space (OTFS) modulation&mdash;two promising technologies poised to play a pivotal role in 6G wireless systems.</span></p>

<p class="mcePastedContent"><span style="font-family: Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif;">This work is the first known attempt to model and optimize STAR-IRS-aided OTFS systems for high-Doppler environments. The proposed approach includes two algorithms that jointly optimize the amplitude and phase of STAR-IRS elements, enabling efficient communication with receivers on both sides of the IRS. Simulation results demonstrate that this system significantly outperforms conventional IRS-based OTFS designs, even when constrained by practical hardware limitations such as 3-bit phase shifts.</span></p>

<p class="mcePastedContent last-child"><span style="font-family: Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif;">Further details can be found in the full paper, which was presented at the 2024 </span><a href="https://ants2024.ieee-ants.org/" target="\_blank"><span style="font-family: Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif;">IEEE International Conference on Advanced Networks and Telecommunications Systems (ANTS).</span></a></p>
