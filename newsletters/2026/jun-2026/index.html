Research Spotlight


We are delighted to share recent research milestones from CNI fellows and faculty member.

CNI Fellow Srinivas Nomula presented his research work titled “Hierarchical Inference with an Offload Queue” at the IEEE INFOCOM 2026, held in Tokyo, Japan, from May 19–21, 2026. This work designs a policy that maximizes overall accuracy while maintaining queue stability in a system with an offload queue attached to an edge device and an edge server connected via a wireless channel. Their policy observes an accuracy-queue-length trade-off while maintaining queue stability across all arrival and service classes.


We are also pleased to share that CNI Fellow Srinivas Nomula's paper, "The Power of Two in Large Service-Marketplaces," has been accepted for publication in IEEE Transactions on Networking (ToN), 2026. The paper studies how simple matching choices in large service marketplaces can significantly improve throughput and revenue, offering new insights into the design of distributed market systems.

 

CNI Fellow Indrasish Chakraborty, together with Prof. Neelesh B. Mehta and Sai Srikar Gollamudi, presented their paper, "Statistics-Aware Low-Overhead Training and Rate Implications for RIS-Aided Systems," at the IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC) 2026, held in Glasgow, UK.

The paper addresses a fundamental challenge in RIS-aided wireless communications, achieving accurate channel estimation while minimizing training overhead. The proposed approach leverages channel statistics to improve estimation efficiency and enhance overall communication performance.

 


We are also delighted to share that Prof. Rajesh Sundaresan's paper "Information Design for Prosocial Behavior," has been published at the prestigious ACM SIGMETRICS. The work talks about how information can be used to encourage socially beneficial behavior in large populations. Using electricity consumption as a case study, it shows that carefully designed feedback—and sometimes even withholding information—can lead to lower overall consumption.
