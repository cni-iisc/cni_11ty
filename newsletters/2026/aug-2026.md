            <p style="font-family:Arial,sans-serif;font-size:13px;color:#333333;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 12px;text-align:justify;">
              We organised the SPARC Workshop on Distributed Learning and Optimization on <strong>13 July 2026</strong> at the ECE Department, IISc Bengaluru. The one-day offline workshop brought together researchers from IITs, IISc, Rutgers University, and Google DeepMind for a focused day of technical exchange.
            </p>
            <p style="font-family:Arial,sans-serif;font-size:13px;color:#333333;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 12px;text-align:justify;">
              The workshop featured eight invited talks spanning distributed learning, optimization, information theory, reinforcement learning, and networked systems. Talks were delivered by <strong>Prof. Chandramani Singh</strong> and <strong>Prof. Sundeep Prabhakar Chepuri</strong> (IISc), <strong>Prof. Kunal Narayan Chowdhury</strong> (IIT Delhi), <strong>Prof. P. N. Karthik</strong> (IIT Hyderabad), <strong>Dr. Kartikeyan Shanmugam</strong> (Google DeepMind), <strong>Prof. Nikhil Karamchandani</strong> (IIT Bombay), <strong>Prof. Amitalok Budkuley</strong> (IIT Kharagpur), and <strong>Prof. Salim El Rouayheb</strong> (Rutgers University).
            </p>
            <p style="font-family:Arial,sans-serif;font-size:13px;color:#333333;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 24px;text-align:justify;">
              The workshop received 147 registrations and was attended by 56 participants in person. The sessions encouraged active discussions between speakers and participants throughout the day.
            </p>

            <p style="font-family:Arial,sans-serif;font-size:13px;color:#333333;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 12px;text-align:justify;">
              We also hosted the <strong>6th CNI Summer School 2026</strong> from <strong>20–24 July 2026</strong> at IISc Bengaluru. The five-day in-person lectures were taught by <strong>Prof. Aditya Mahajan</strong>, Professor at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, McGill University.
            </p>
            <p style="font-family:Arial,sans-serif;font-size:13px;color:#333333;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 12px;text-align:justify;">
              This year's theme was <strong>Model Approximation in MDPs and POMDPs</strong>. The course introduced a unified approach to look at Markov Decision Processes with full and partially observations, and explored how model approximation can be applied to decision-making under uncertainty. Topics ranged from dynamic programming and Bellman operators to approximation bounds and their connections to reinforcement learning.
            </p>
            <p style="font-family:Arial,sans-serif;font-size:13px;color:#333333;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 24px;text-align:justify;">
              The summer school saw strong participation, with <strong>89 participants</strong> attending in person from 185 eligible registrations. Participants included PhD, Master's, and Bachelor's students, faculty members, postdoctoral researchers, and industry professionals from institutions across India including IISc, IIT Madras, IIT Roorkee, and several NITs and IIITs.
            </p>
